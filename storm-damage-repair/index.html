Storm Damage Repair Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Trim trees near your home to lower the risk of wind-driven branch damage
  • If storm damage breaks a pipe you will need the main shutoff, so find it and practice now
  • Homes in hurricane country benefit from shutters or impact-rated window glass
  • Reseal worn caulk and weatherstripping around windows and doors before the rainy season
  • Good attic insulation and ventilation stop ice dams from forming along the roof edge
  • For insurance purposes, keep a yearly set of pictures showing the state of your property

How to Tell You Need Storm Damage Repair

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Damp, earthy odors that appear a few days after the storm
  • Sagging or detached gutters that spill rainwater down the siding
  • Metal flashing pulled away from vents, valleys or the edge of the roof
  • Bubbling paint and soft, drooping spots overhead once the rain stops
  • Flooding in lower levels because drainage systems were overwhelmed
  • Water getting past doors, windows and wall penetrations when wind pushes the rain
  • Missing, damaged or lifted shingles you can see from the ground

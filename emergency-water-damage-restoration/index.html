How to Tell You Need Emergency Water Damage Restoration

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Floor-level power strips, plugged-in appliances or the breaker panel are now wet.
  • What is on the floor came up from the sewer, or it is discolored and smells foul.
  • You see water escaping around a light fitting, from a wall outlet or through the ceiling.
  • Gallons keep spilling from a ruptured water heater tank.
  • Water from a floor above is streaming down interior walls.
  • Nobody was home when it started, so the length of the leak is unknown.
  • Standing water is spreading across floors toward other rooms or down the stairs.

How to Prevent Emergency Water Damage Restoration Problems

Taking preventive measures can help you avoid costly water damage issues down the road. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Keep the number of an emergency restoration company saved in your phone before you need it.
  • Put leak alarms beneath sinks and beside the dishwasher, washer and water heater.
  • Know where your electrical panel is so power to wet rooms can be cut safely.
  • Swap worn hoses feeding the washer, sinks and toilets rather than waiting for a rupture.
  • Any trip beyond a few days is a reason to turn the main supply off before you go.
  • Every member of the household should know where the main water valve is and how it closes.

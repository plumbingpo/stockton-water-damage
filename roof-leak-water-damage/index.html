Dealing With Roof Leak Water Damage in Stockton

Where you see the stain and where the rain gets in are usually two different places. A loose shingle, a failed flashing joint or an aging seal around a skylight lets rain in, and it travels across the roof deck and framing until it falls into the insulation. A ceiling stain tends to arrive late, well after the space overhead first got wet. We find the full wet area and dry it out.

Our trade is water damage restoration, and roofing is a separate one. Our job is the inside of the house: pulling out soaked insulation, drying the framing and drywall, treating for mold and putting the ceiling back together. When the roof is still letting water through, a temporary tarp is something we can install, and we will coordinate with your roofer so the repair comes before the ceiling is closed.

Do This First

What to Do Right Now

  1. Shut Off Wet CircuitsTurn off the breaker for any light, fan or outlet that has water running through or near it, then keep your hands off it.
  2. Catch the DripsSet buckets or pans under active drips and move furniture, rugs and electronics out of the way. Plastic or towels spread on the floor will keep it from soaking.
  3. Relieve a Ceiling BulgeWhen water pools in a bubble overhead, pierce the low point with a screwdriver while standing clear, and let it run into a bucket.
  4. Stay Off the RoofClimbing on slick shingles or crawling around a dark attic is not worth the risk. From indoors, mark where the water enters, and let professionals handle everything else.
  5. Photograph the DamageTake photos and video of the ceiling, the attic and any damaged belongings before you move or throw anything away, then call us to start drying.
The Usual Culprits

How It Usually Starts

  • Clogged gutters that back water up under the shingles at the roof edge.
  • A skylight whose curb or seal has dried and cracked, so rain gets past it.
  • Holes and fractures left in the roof after a branch or hailstones hit it.
  • Leaves and grit collecting where two roof slopes meet, damming rain until it seeps through.
  • Corroded or loose metal flashing where the roof meets a chimney, a vent or a wall.
If It Is Left

Where the Damage Spreads

Roof Framing

Repeated wetting makes decking, trusses and rafters turn dark, swell up and go soft. Decay here means less strength in what carries the roof.

Inside the Walls

Water that reaches the top of a wall runs down inside it, wetting insulation and the back of the drywall. You might notice nothing more than a peeling patch or a puffy baseboard.

Mold in the Attic

A dark, warm attic with damp wood and paper-faced drywall suits mold well. Within 24 to 48 hours it can start growing, then move across the bottom of the roof deck.

Insulation in the Attic

For days after a leak, matted insulation sits wet on top of the ceiling and no longer insulates. Fiberglass can sometimes be dried. Cellulose that is saturated generally comes out.

Insurance

What About Insurance?

Interior damage from a sudden, accidental roof leak, like one caused by a fallen limb or wind-torn shingles, is often covered under a homeowners policy. Leaks blamed on age, wear or skipped maintenance are usually denied, and the roof repair itself may be handled differently from the interior. Our team gives your adjuster photos and moisture readings, yet what is covered comes down to your policy, so check it or talk to your agent.

Pricing

How the Quote Is Built

  • How long the roof leaked before it was found, and any mold growth.
  • Damage beyond the ceiling, such as wet floors, wall cavities or furniture.
  • How much repainting is required so the patched ceiling blends in.
  • Drywall removal: how much of the ceiling and walls is beyond saving.

Our Process

What Happens After You Call

1

Map Where Water Went

Moisture meters and a thermal camera let us follow the rain through the attic, the ceilings and the walls, so spots that look dry from underneath are not missed.

2

Cover and Protect

When rain can still get in, a temporary tarp from us or a call to your roofer comes first. Floors and belongings below the wet ceiling are covered as well.

3

Tear Out and Dry

Unsalvageable drywall and insulation come out, and dehumidifiers and air movers go into the attic space and each room under it, with readings taken until the framing has dried.

4

Treat and Rebuild

Where it is called for, we apply an antimicrobial to the bare framing. Once the roof repair is done, we replace insulation, hang and finish new drywall, then prime and paint to match the ceiling.
